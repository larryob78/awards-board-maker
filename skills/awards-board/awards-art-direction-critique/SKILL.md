# Art direction critique

## Inputs

Read the [shared contract](../shared/CONTRACT.md). Use the rendered board with element/region IDs, the approved fact and asset snapshot, user intent, locked choices, target viewing conditions and prior decisions. If there is no render, limit the review to structure/copy and mark visual judgement Not checked.

## Reasoning

- First restate the intended idea and what the current board communicates. Separate campaign gaps, factual risks and presentation issues; do not repeatedly redesign around a missing outcome.
- Prioritise at most three consequential issues. For each, identify the exact text or region, explain the reader consequence and propose a bounded alternative. “More premium” or “more dynamic” alone is not an actionable critique.
- Show before and proposed after, with annotations anchored to persistent element IDs and version-specific regions. State which facts, layout dependencies and brand choices change. Protect user locks; request an explicit override rather than silently ignoring them.
- Assess distinctiveness through fit to this particular mechanism and brand. Challenge stock visual metaphors, generic headlines and interchangeable structures. Do not claim originality clearance or compare against uncleared references. Repetition across the app's own permitted examples may inform a recommendation, not a universal ban.
- Offer optional comprehension exercises: about five seconds to identify the idea, a short read to explain the mechanism, and a deep read to evaluate proof. Record questions, audience, viewing conditions and actual responses if tests are run. These are proposed exercises, not jury timing rules or eye tracking.
- Reassess after a selected change rather than accumulating endless suggestions. Stop when the agreed task is resolved or remaining issues require missing facts or user decisions.

## Output and UI

Return `priority_issues` with severity in words, target, observed problem, reason, before/after proposal and tradeoff; a `preserve` list; and any proposed comprehension test. Separate observed findings, model predictions and actual human test results.

The UI highlights each affected region and allows accept/edit/reject plus undo. A before/after preview is generated by the renderer and labelled proposed. An accepted edit becomes a new version, never a destructive replacement.

Include logo authenticity, placement and rendered/exported clarity in review. Do not claim a pass from a file extension or invent a replacement mark. Refer unresolved logo quality to the brand/logo skill; the app must enforce final-quality blocking while draft saves remain available.

## Quality checks

Can a developer apply each suggestion unambiguously? Does it preserve approved facts and intent? Are speculative benefits labelled as predictions? Do not invent reviewer responses, attention maps or jury preferences. No automated score certifies world-class quality; unresolved uncertainty stays visible.
