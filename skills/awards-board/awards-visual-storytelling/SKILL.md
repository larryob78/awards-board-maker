# Visual storytelling

Read the [shared contract](../shared/CONTRACT.md). Work from the central argument, source-linked facts, permitted assets, shared brand constraints and final canvas. Identify whether observations come from inspected pixels, supplied descriptions or an existing render. Unavailable pixels cannot support precise crop coordinates or claims of inspection.

## Make meaning visible

State what the reader must understand: who encounters the intervention, what changes in that encounter, and how the mechanism works. Select the hero for that explanatory job. A beautiful scene that hides the intervention is weak evidence; a modest interaction detail may carry the argument. Explain the choice in terms of visible relationships, not brightness scores or image area.

Choose the smallest useful sequence: context/action/detail, before/after, or a system diagram. A before/after comparison requires documented states and comparable context; do not imply causation from juxtaposition. Each supporting visual should resolve a question the hero leaves open. Keep required client assets and surface any conflict with clarity.

When pixels and permission allow, propose detail crops showing the meaningful interaction, readable instruction or material feature. Preserve enough surrounding context to explain scale, participants and location. Distinguish a crop from a retouch or reconstruction. Never crop away qualifiers or join unrelated moments into an apparent event. With descriptions alone, offer conditional crop intent and request the source before specifying boundaries.

Keep `documented_execution`, `diagram`, `mockup`, `ai_concept` and `unknown` explicit. Illustrative diagrams explain sourced relationships; mockups and AI concepts communicate proposed form. None proves deployment, audience behaviour or results. Bind captions to facts and keep required labels visible in export.

## Compose the final board

Evaluate the hero with headline, explanatory copy, evidence and reading order. Recommend scale and placement that make the mechanism understandable at the intended viewing distance. Reserve approved-logo placement using the shared brand constraints, authentic asset ID, clear space and permitted background; never invent or redraw an identity. Flag missing logo assets or unknown rules.

Return the contract envelope with `hero_brief`, `supporting_sequence`, `crop_proposals`, `missing_asset_requests` and board-level relationships. Include asset IDs, demonstration goal, fact bindings, labels, tradeoffs and conditional checks. Route original concept briefs to [visual prompts](../awards-visual-prompts/SKILL.md); a proposal authorises no generation.

Review semantic clarity separately from render checks. Verify actual crop integrity, effective resolution, caption and label legibility, logo clear space and visual hierarchy only against an available target render. Mark unavailable checks Not checked; preserve existing assets and crops as recoverable versions.
