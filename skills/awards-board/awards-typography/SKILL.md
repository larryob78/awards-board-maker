# Typography

## Inputs

Read the [shared contract](../shared/CONTRACT.md). Use approved copy, locked strings, brand voice, text roles, canvas/viewing conditions, available font files, licence records and version-specific renderer measurements. Include the approved logo placement and supplied clearspace rules. Installed, loaded and licensed are different states; record evidence for each.

## Reasoning

- Coordinate campaign title, explanatory headline, body, captions, labels and evidence through scale, weight, measure and spacing. Match emphasis to the argument: display treatment may clarify a short idea but weaken dense qualifications. Add another family only when differentiation helps and actual glyph coverage supports the copy.
- Shape headline breaks around meaning; keep units with values and qualifications with claims. Treat leading, tracking, line length, paragraph spacing and alignment as a connected system. Use rendered texture and viewing distance, not universal ratios or an assumed minimum body size.
- Preserve authentic logo geometry. Align surrounding type optically with its visible mark while respecting supplied clearspace and minimum-size rules. Do not rebuild a wordmark from a similar font, change its letterspacing or stretch it to match a headline. Route missing assets, unknown variants or authenticity concerns to [brand/logo](../awards-brand-logo/SKILL.md); typography does not certify them.
- Resolve crowding through proposed removal of repetition, shortening without factual loss, recomposition and changed emphasis before smaller type. Preserve user locks and essential qualifiers; explain any copy change separately from spacing. Request a needed override rather than silently changing a locked choice.
- Verify the selected face and weight after loading; expose fallback. Check embedding/export permission separately. Unknown licensing requires resolution or a supplied cleared alternative. Use actual shaping capabilities; tracking controls do not provide pair-level kerning.

## Outputs and UI

Return the shared envelope containing `type_roles`, font candidates with installed/load/licence states, target-specific line breaks, spacing/alignment proposals, rationale, copy/layout consequences and `renderer_checks_needed`. Include logo-clearspace dependencies without duplicating asset-authenticity findings.

Show coordinated presets alongside editable controls, actual-font previews and accept/edit/reject. State fallback and overflow in words as well as colour. Preserve originals and user overrides; a selected change creates a recoverable version. Display final-quality blockers from unresolved logo checks while allowing draft saving; this describes required future app behaviour, not enforcement by this file.

## Quality checks

Inspect actual glyphs, clipping, baselines, crowded lines, widows, captions and source notes after fonts load. Measure containers and inspect the exported board at intended size, including logo/type separation. Without rendering, mark appearance and fit Not checked. Geometry establishes fit; actual human review tests comprehension. Unknown viewing conditions remain explicit.
